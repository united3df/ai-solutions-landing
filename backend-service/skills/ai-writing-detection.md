# AI Writing Detection — Avoid These Patterns

## Banned Words and Phrases

Do NOT use:
- delve
- leverage
- robust
- furthermore
- moreover
- utilize (use "use" instead)
- plethora
- myriad
- seamless
- cutting-edge (overused)
- game-changer

## Banned Punctuation

- NO em-dash (—) — use comma, period, or parentheses instead

## Banned Openings

Do NOT start with:
- "In today's..."
- "In the realm of..."
- "Let's delve..."
- "In this article, we will..."
- "It's no secret that..."

## Banned Closings

Do NOT end with:
- "In conclusion..."
- "To sum up..."
- "In summary..."
- "All in all..."

## Style

- Write like a human, not an AI assistant
- Prefer short sentences
- Avoid filler and hedging ("it's important to note that...")
- Be direct and specific
