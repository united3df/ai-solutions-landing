# AI SEO Guidelines

## Topic Evaluation Criteria

1. **Search intent** — Is there real user demand? (informational, commercial, navigational)
2. **Competition** — Is it too competitive for a new site?
3. **Topic fit** — Does it match the site theme and target audience?

## Keyword Density

- Target: 1-2% (not under 1%, not over 2%)
- Avoid keyword stuffing
- Use natural variations and LSI terms

## Meta Requirements

- Meta title: 50-60 characters, keyword included
- Meta description: 140-155 characters, keyword included, compelling CTA

## Content Quality Signals

- Definition block or direct answer in first paragraph
- H2/H3 structure matches search queries
- FAQ block or self-contained paragraphs for AI citation
- Word count: 900-1200 for blog posts

## Scoring (1-10)

- 9-10: Excellent fit, low competition, clear intent
- 7-8: Good fit, manageable competition
- 5-6: Marginal, needs improvement
- 1-4: Poor fit, high competition, or weak intent
